## CNIC OVERVIEW

Computerised National Identity Card (CNIC) is an identity document issued by NADRA under Section 9 & 10 of NADRA Ordinance, 2000. This serves as official proof of a citizen's identity, nationality and legal status within Pakistan.

Types of CNIC:
- CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card): Standard Teslin-based identity card issued to adult citizens.
- Smart CNIC: An advanced card with a secure microchip, enhanced security features, and improved durability.
- CNIC with QR-code: A newer version of CNIC containing a machine-readable QR-code to facilitate faster verification and authentication of the cardholder's data.

## NEW CNIC — APPLY FOR NEW CNIC

Eligibility:
CNIC is issued to all Pakistani Citizens in or out of Pakistan, who has attained the age of 18 years and has either not been previously registered with NADRA or is a holder of a Child Registration Certificate (CRC) / Juvenile Card.

Requirements:
- Identity card number of parent(s) or blood relative(s) OR Biometric witness by an ID holder along with undertaking "B" (as per format) if applicant has no blood relative (for System Independent cases only)
- Computerised Birth certificate (verified) of UC / Municipal Committee / Cantonment or Citizenship / Naturalization certificate
- Biometric verification of Parent or Blood relative OR Attestation of CNIC as per Regulation 9 (a-h)

Process of applying through NADRA Registration Centre:
1. Token Acquisition — Obtain a Queue Matic token upon arrival.
2. Biometric Capture — Digital capture of live photograph and fingerprints.
3. Data Entry — Personal information is entered into the system and reviewed for accuracy.
4. Application Attestation — Attestation by anyone of the parent, sibling, or a Gazetted Officer.
5. OIC Interview / Approval — A brief interview conducted by the Officer-In-Charge.
6. Payment — Submission of the required processing fee.
7. Card Printing — Card is printed.

Process of applying through PakID App:
- Download the PakID mobile app
- Create your account or sign in
- Select "New CNIC"
- Fill in your personal details
- Upload required documents
- Visit a NADRA Registration Centre (if required)
- Track your application status in the app

## CNIC MODIFICATION — COMMONLY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (SECONDARY SOURCE — NOT OFFICIALLY PUBLISHED BY NADRA)

NADRA's own CNIC page does not publish a specific document checklist for modification cases — it only refers applicants to the "Change / Correction in Identity Documents" chapter of the NADRA Registration Policy, which is not itself published in accessible form on the public site. The following is compiled from multiple independent third-party guides (not NADRA directly) describing what NADRA Registration Centres commonly request in practice. Treat this tier as practical guidance, not an official guarantee — a citizen should still confirm requirements at the counter, and the system should represent this section with lower confidence than officially-sourced sections above.

By modification type, commonly requested documents:
- **Name correction/change:** A name-change affidavit (on prescribed stamp paper) attested by an Oath Commissioner; the applicant's old CNIC/NICOP; biometric verification of the applicant together with a blood relative at the NADRA centre (or attestation by a Gazetted Officer if no blood relative is available); supporting documents such as a Nikahnama (marriage certificate) for a name change due to marriage, or a matriculation/educational certificate if correcting a spelling error to match other official records.
- **Address change:** Original CNIC plus proof of the new address — a utility bill, rent agreement, or property ownership documents in the applicant's or an immediate family member's name.
- **Date of birth correction:** Union Council-issued Birth Certificate and/or B-Form (Child Registration Certificate); if the applicant holds education beyond matriculation, matching correction of DOB on those educational certificates may also be requested.
- **Birthplace correction:** Original CNIC, original Union Council birth certificate, and original B-Form.

## NICOP OVERVIEW (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis)

National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis (NICOP) is an identity document issued by NADRA under Section 12 of NADRA Ordinance, 2000 to Pakistani citizens residing abroad. NICOP serves as official proof of identity and nationality for Pakistanis living overseas and allows visa-free entry into Pakistan.

Types of NICOP:
- NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis): Standard Teslin-based NICOP. Currently, new Teslin-based NICOPs are not being issued.
- Smart NICOP: An advanced card with a secure microchip, enhanced security features, and improved durability.
- NICOP with QR-code: A newer version containing a machine-readable QR-code for faster verification.

## NEW NICOP

Eligibility — NICOP is issued to Pakistani citizens who are residing abroad and meet any of the following conditions:
- Pakistani citizens living or working outside Pakistan
- Pakistani citizens holding dual nationality of a country recognized by the Government of Pakistan
- Pakistani citizens who intend to stay abroad for an extended period and require proof of identity and nationality
- Children of Pakistani citizens born abroad who are eligible to be registered as Pakistani citizens

Requirements:
- Identity card number of parent(s) or blood relative(s) OR Biometric witness by an ID holder along with undertaking "B" (as per format) if applicant has no blood relative (for System Independent cases only)
- Computerised Birth certificate (verified) of UC / Municipal Committee / Cantonment or Citizenship / Naturalization certificate (For non-residents)
- Foreign detailed Birth certificate or manual Birth certificate or SI form issued by Embassy / Mission or Citizenship or Naturalization certificate (For non-residents)
- Pakistani / Foreign passport or residence permit or Travel document or Undertaking "A" duly attested by Pakistan Embassy / Mission — Refer clauses (d & e) of Rule 8 (4) (For non-residents)
- Biometric verification of Parent or Blood relative OR Attestation of CNIC as per Regulation 9 (a-h) (For inland DAUs only) OR Verification of Application Form by any ID holder (For applicants applying from abroad only)

## FAMILY REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE (FRC)

Family Registration Certificate means a certificate issued by the Authority which reflects, verifies and provides a record of the family composition data of a registered person. FRC is a document issued by NADRA under Rule 19 of the NADRA (NIC) Rules, 2002. FRC serves as valid proof of family composition data and is generally used for immigration, visa processing and other legal or administrative purposes.

Types of FRC:
- By Birth: Includes details of parents and siblings.
- By Marriage: Includes details of spouse and children.
- By Adoption: Includes details of guardian and adopted family members.
- By All: Includes details of both alpha and beta family members.

Eligibility:
To be eligible for a FRC, all family members should be registered with NADRA and have a valid 13-digit identification number. However, unregistered deceased and alive (in certain scenarios) individuals can also be included. Particulars of such family members are mentioned as per information provided by the applicant.

Requirements:
- 13-digit ID number of the applicant (CRC / Juvenile Card / CNIC / NICOP / POC)

Process of applying through NADRA Registration Centre:
1. Token Acquisition — Obtain a Queue Matic token upon arrival.
2. Biometric Verification — Biometric verification of applicant and children (if applicable).
3. Photo Capture — Photographs of children (if the CRC was issued without a photograph).
4. Data Retrieval and Review — Data will be downloaded and reviewed for accuracy.
5. Payment — Submission of the required processing fee.
6. FRC Printing — FRC is printed.
