## OVERVIEW

Every citizen of Pakistan is entitled to a Machine Readable Passport (MRP) for traveling abroad, subject to completion of all codal (legal/procedural) formalities. Under the revised Passport Rule 6, 2021: every citizen may apply for passport services from any Passport Office irrespective of the address recorded on their CNIC/NICOP/CRC — applicants are not restricted to a specific regional office based on their registered address.

Passport types: Ordinary Passport (Machine Readable Passport / MRP — the standard passport most citizens use), e-Passport (biometric passport, launched 2022, with an embedded chip), Official Passport (for government officials), Diplomatic Passport, and Emergency Travel Document (ETD, issued by a Pakistani Mission abroad for citizens who need to urgently return to Pakistan without their passport).

## GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

MRP applications are processed using data from NADRA's system — a valid CNIC, NICOP, Smart National ID Card, or CRC is required before an MRP application can be processed. If an applicant does not have a valid one of these documents, they must obtain it from NADRA first.

Documents commonly required (adults):
- Original valid CNIC (or NICOP/Smart NIC for the applicable category)
- Married female applicants must have their husband's name reflected on their CNIC/NICOP/Smart NIC — otherwise the ID must be modified first, before applying for the MRP
- Government employees require an original No Objection Certificate (NOC) issued within the last 90 days by their Ministry/Division/Department
- Dual nationals applying for their first-ever Pakistani passport are subject to agency verification

Documents commonly required (minors, under 18):
- Both parents' original CNICs/NICOPs and passports (or the legal guardian's, with a court-issued guardianship certificate if applicable)
- Detailed birth certificate showing both parents' names
- If parents are separated/divorced, court orders confirming legal custody

Passport page and validity options: MRPs are available in 36, 72, or 100 pages, with 5-year or 10-year validity. Additional pages beyond 36 attract an extra fee (see fee section). Children under 15 are not eligible for 10-year validity and must be issued the 5-year validity passport.

## APPLICATION PROCESS (AT A REGIONAL PASSPORT OFFICE / NADRA MEGA CENTRE)

1. **Token acquisition** — arrive and obtain a token; documents are checked at the MRP counter.
2. **Fee payment** — if documents are complete, a bank voucher/PSID is issued for fee payment (can also be paid in advance online via the Passport Fee Asaan app or web portal).
3. **Biometric capture** — digital photograph and fingerprint capture (right thumb, right index, left thumb, left index) once the applicant's token is called.
4. **Data entry** — operator enters application data; a printed Data Entry Form is presented to the applicant for verification and signature.
5. **Clearance check** — verification against the Exit Control List (ECL) and Black List (BL); if a person is on either list, processing stops until removal from the list per applicable rules.
6. **Interview/decision** — the Assistant Director/Officer-in-Charge interviews the applicant to confirm national status and credibility, and either approves ("Go ahead") or requests additional documents or a security-agency verification referral.
7. **Production and dispatch** — once approved, the passport is produced and dispatched; the applicant is notified via SMS (inland, via service 9988) or email (overseas, via Email Rabta service).
8. **Collection** — applicants must collect their passport in person (one family member may collect for the whole family). Passports not collected within a month of the notified collection date are destroyed per policy.
9. **Post-collection requirement** — each holder must sign or provide a thumb impression on page 2 of the passport immediately after collection (for minors: right thumb for female, left thumb for male).

## ONLINE APPLICATION (INLAND RENEWAL / OVERSEAS PAKISTANIS)

Available via the e-Services Portal (onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk), for users registered on the Pak-Identity system.
- **Inland online applicants** can currently apply for **renewal only**; the renewed passport is delivered to their selected Passport Office (not home delivery, for inland applicants).
- **Overseas online applicants** have broader access: Renewal, New (for newborns up to 2 years of age only), Reprint (Damaged), Reprint (Lost — limited to 1st and 2nd loss only; a 3rd loss requires an in-person visit to the Foreign Mission), and Modification (marital status, name, or profession change for female or male applicants).
- Biometric verification for online applicants can now be completed via the NADRA PakID mobile app at the fingerprint-upload stage, replacing manual fingerprint capture in many cases.
- Applications may require re-submission of fingerprint forms up to 3 times if rejected for quality reasons; after 3 consecutive rejections, the online system withholds issuance and the applicant must visit their nearest RPO/Foreign Mission instead.
- Online passport fee, once paid, is **non-refundable and non-transferable** to another person.
- Applicants can convert an existing Normal-category online application to Urgent by paying the fee difference, before the application is finalized.

|---|---|---|
| Normal Passport | Rs. 4,500 | 21 working days |
| Urgent Passport | Rs. 7,500 | 5 working days |
| Fast Track (Executive Passport Offices) | — (see note below) | 2 working days |

The above 36-page figures are directly confirmed via DGIP's own FAQ page. Fast Track / Executive Passport Office service is available at designated offices in major cities (e.g., Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi) for the fastest turnaround, but this corpus does not have an official-source-confirmed Fast Track fee figure for 36 pages specifically.

## LOST OR DAMAGED PASSPORT

- **Lost passport:** a police report must be lodged with the concerned police station first; a lost-passport MRP application will only be delivered after verification of the previous MRP/manual passport from the original issuing authority, which the DGIP's own site notes "may take a month or longer."
- **Damaged passport:** replacement is issued subject to the prescribed fee and production of required documents (including the damaged passport itself).
- **Pages exhausted:** frequent travelers whose passport pages are full may apply for a fresh passport with a higher page count (72 or 100 pages), subject to the applicable extra fee.

## NAME / DATE OF BIRTH / PHOTOGRAPH CHANGES (Confirmed via official DGIP FAQ)

- **Name change:** submit the revised original CNIC reflecting the updated name — the passport is updated to match the CNIC, not the other way around.
- **Date of birth change:** submit the revised original CNIC reflecting the updated DOB; a birth certificate or matriculation certificate may additionally be requested for verification.
- **Photograph update:** requires issuance of an entirely new passport, per policy — cannot be updated on the existing booklet.
- **Name/DOB changes altogether (multiple changes at once):** forwarded by the Regional Passport Office/Foreign Mission to DGIP Headquarters, Islamabad for approval — this is a slower process than a single, isolated change.

## CHILDREN'S PASSPORTS

- Payment for a child's passport fee uses the child's own 13-digit registration number (from B-Form/FRC or Smart CNIC/NICOP), not the parent's.
- Children under 15 are not entitled to 10-year validity and must be charged/issued the 5-year validity fee.
- A newborn's passport (Type "New," online, overseas applicants only) can be processed only up to 2 years of age; beyond that, an in-person visit to the Foreign Mission with supporting documents is required.
